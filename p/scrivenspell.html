ScrivenSpell

Posted on 11/7/2026
Tags: Programming, Games
Introducing ScrivenSpell, a word game with RPG elements.

In ScrivenSpell, make words as quickly as possible to fight monsters. Longer words get additional points. When you defeat a monster, a stronger one takes its place.

Unlock Quote Quest for an extra challenge and try to achieve 100% completion!


This game is inspired by Up Spell. That game was a lot of fun but unfortunately no longer fits the screen shapes of recent devices.

It also lacked staying power, at least for me, which is why I experimented with RPG elements in ScrivenSpell. The monsters and Quote Quest give the player a goal and something to complete.
